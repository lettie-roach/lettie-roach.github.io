Research

I am broadly interested in Earth's climate system, with a focus on the polar regions. My research aims to better understand the physics of coupled interactions between ice and climate. I typically work with climate models of varying levels of complexity, in combination with observations, aiming to improve climate projections. My specific interests include marginal ice zone physics, historical and future sea ice change and ice sheet-climate interactions. Currently, I have a junior research group on the topic of Southern Ocean and Antarctic sea Ice Evolution.
